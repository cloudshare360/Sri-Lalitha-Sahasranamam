# Samputikarana Inputs and Stanza Guide for 8 Life Areas

This guide fulfills two tasks grounded in Sri Guru Karunamaya's documented teachings ([04 — Samputikarana process and English transcripts](04-Samputikarana_Process_and_Transcripts.md)) and this repository's canonical text ([01 — Transcription](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md) / [02 — PDF](02-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.pdf)):

1. **Inventory of Samputikarana inputs** attributed to Sri Guru Karunamaya in the cited sources.
2. **Stanza and line recommendations across the 8 requested life areas**, separating **Guru Karunamaya's explicitly published video/channel selections** from **additional canonical stanzas identified by thematic research** in the 183-stanza text.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Source separation:** Verbatim lines are quoted in the source edition's nonstandard romanization (`docs/01-...` / `docs/02-...`). Standardized Sanskrit / IAST spellings and thematic interpretations are derived material, not part of the source transcription.
>
> **Video coverage:** [03 — Video index](03-Guru_Karunamaya_Samputikarana.md) links identified line-specific videos. No line-specific Guru Karunamaya Samputikarana video has been identified in this guide for the thematic selections `143/2`, `119/1`, `144/2`, `159/1`, `134/1`, `136/1`, `165/1`, `135/1`, `175/2`, `55/1`, `69/1`, `158/2`, `111/1`, `97/2`, `50/2`, `61/2`, `85/1`, `169/1`, `157/2`, `171/2`, and `183/1`. This does not establish that videos do not exist. The [general method explanation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrmBAEpK9DA) is not a line-specific recitation.
>
> Spiritual or health-related claims are attributed to their sources, not medically verified. These practices are not a substitute for medical or mental health care.
