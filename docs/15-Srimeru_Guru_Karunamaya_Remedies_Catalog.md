# Sri Guru Karunamaya (srimeru.org): Complete Remedies Catalog

Catalog of **all 16 remedies** published on the *Srividya Jnaana Peetham* remedies page — [srimeru.org/remedies](https://srimeru.org/remedies/) — associated with Sri Guru Karunamaya's teaching lineage. Each remedy is mapped to its prescribed **Lalitha Sahasranamam stanza** (where one is used) and cross-verified against this repository's authoritative edition ([01 — Transcription](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md) / [02 — PDF](02-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.pdf)).

> **Scope note:** This catalog captures Guru Karunamaya's *condition-indexed* remedies from the website. It is distinct from [13](13-Nanaji_Patnaik_Consolidated_Solutions_Guide.md)/[14](14-Nanaji_Patnaik_Complete_Remedies_Reference.md), which cover **Nanaji Patnaik's** video prescriptions. Several srimeru remedies use scriptures *other than* the Lalitha Sahasranamam (Sri Rudram, Durga Saptashati, Medha Suktham, varahi/gandharva mantras, homas, and pujas); those are marked **non-LSN** and have no stanza entry.

> **Source fidelity:** The website labels each audio file with a romanized stanza fragment (often slightly different from this edition's spelling). Where a stanza is used, the **transcription line here is authoritative**; the website audio filename is given for traceability. Health claims are spiritual, not medically verified, and are not a substitute for professional care.

## 2. Lalitha Sahasranamam Stanza Remedies (Detailed)

### Remedy 1 & 15 — Skin Disease / Skin Ailments
- **Stanza:** **99/1** — `Payasanna priya tvaksdha pashuloka bhayankari`
- **Full stanza 99:** `Payasanna priya tvaksdha pashuloka bhayankari / Amrutadi mahashakti sanvruta dakinishvari – 99`
- **PDF:** p. 11, [Line 359](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L359)
- **Derived IAST:** *Payasānnapriyā tvaksthitā paśuloka-bhayaṅkarī*
- **Meaning:** *"She who delights in sweet rice-offering (payasanna), who abides in the skin (tvak-sthā), and who is the awe-inspiring protectress of the animal world."* `Tvak-sthā` (abiding in the skin) makes this the traditional skin-disease selection.
- **Website audio:** `payasannapriya-tvakstha-pasuloka-bhayaṅkari.mp3`, `skin-disease.mp3`, `payasannapriya-pasuloka-bhayaṅkari.mp3`
- **Companion physical method (Guru Karunamaya, from [05](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md)):** Mix **sandalwood oil + turmeric + tulsi-leaf juice + honey**; apply gently **downward only** (never rub back and forth) while listening to the recitation.
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/skin-disease](https://srimeru.org/skin-disease/) · [srimeru.org/skin-ailments](https://srimeru.org/skin-ailments/)

### Remedy 2 — Paralysis (Peralysis)
- **Stanza:** **170/1** — `Chaitanyardhya samaradhya chaitanya kusumapriya`
- **Full stanza 170:** `Chaitanyardhya samaradhya chaitanya kusumapriya / Sadodita sadatushta tarunadityapatala – 170`
- **PDF:** p. 17, [Line 584](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L584)
- **Derived IAST:** *Caitanyārdhyā samārādhyā caitanya-kusumapriyā*
- **Meaning:** *"She who is worshipped with the offering of consciousness (caitanya), who is propitiated by the flower of pure awareness."* Invoked for restoring the flow of *caitanya* (conscious vitality) through the nervous/motor system.
- **Website audio:** `chaitanyarghya-samaradhya-chaitanya-kusumapriya.mp3`, `paralysis-1.mp3` (note site spells it "chaitanyarghya"; source reads "Chaitanyardhya")
- **Companion physical method (from [05](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md)):** Same **sandalwood oil + turmeric + tulsi-leaf juice** paste **without honey**, applied gently downward while listening.
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/peralysis](https://srimeru.org/peralysis/)

### Remedy 5 — Marriage for Girls
- **Stanza:** **12/2** — `Kamesha bada mangalya sutra-shobhita kandhara`
- **Full stanza 12:** `Anakalita sadrusya chubuka shree virajita / Kamesha bada mangalya sutra-shobhita kandhara – 12`
- **PDF:** p. 4, [Line 85](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L85)
- **Derived IAST:** *Kāmeśa-baddha-māṅgalya-sūtra-śobhita-kandharā* (often cited as `Kameshabadha`; source prints `Kamesha bada`)
- **Meaning:** *"Whose neck is adorned with the sacred mangalsutra tied by Lord Kameshwara."*
- **Website audio:** `Kamesha-baddha-mangalya-sutra-shobhita-kandhara.mp3`, `Marriage-for-Girls.m4a`
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/marriage-for-girls](https://srimeru.org/marriage-for-girls/)

### Remedy 7 — Knee Pain
- **Stanza:** **17/2** — `Manikya makuta kara janudvaya virajita`
- **Full stanza 17:** `Kameshagyata saobhagya mardavoru dvayanvita / Manikya makuta kara janudvaya virajita – 17`
- **PDF:** p. 4, [Line 100](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L100)
- **Derived IAST:** *Māṇikya-makuṭākāra-jānudvaya-virājitā*
- **Meaning:** *"Whose twin knees shine like crowns of radiant rubies."* The literal `janu` (knee) correspondence makes this the knee-specific selection (the same stanza also serves career/10th-house in Nanaji Patnaik's method — see [07](07-Stanzas_Prescribed_by_Nanaji_Patnaik.md)).
- **Website audio:** `Manikya-makuta-kara-janudvaya-virajita.mp3`, `knee-pain-english.mp4`
- **Companion physical method (from [05](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md)):** Listen/chant while **crisscrossing both hands over the knees**.
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/knee-pain](https://srimeru.org/knee-pain/)

### Remedy 9 — Not Having Kids (Infertility)
- **Stanza:** **109/2** — `Sarvayudhadharashukla sansdhita sarvatomukhi`
- **Full stanza 109:** `Sahasradala padmasdha sarvavarnopashobhita / Sarvayudhadharashukla sansdhita sarvatomukhi – 109`
- **PDF:** p. 12, [Line 392](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L392)
- **Derived IAST:** *Sarvāyudhadharā śuklā saṃsthitā sarvatomukhī*
- **Meaning:** *"Holding all weapons, white/radiant (śuklā), established everywhere, facing all directions (sarvatomukhī)."* Recited in the Sahasrara-padma context (the preceding line), traditionally invoked for progeny and overall auspiciousness.
- **Website audio:** `sarvayudhadhara-sukla-saṃsthita-sarvatomukhi.mp3`, `Not-having-kids-english-1.mp4`
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/not-having-kids](https://srimeru.org/not-having-kids/)

### Remedy 10 — Job, Business
- **Stanza:** **53/2** — `Mahishvari mahadivi mahalakshmi rmrudapriya`
- **Full stanza 53:** (see [05](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md)) — `Mahishvari mahadivi mahalakshmi rmrudapriya – 53`
- **PDF:** p. 7, [Line 214](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L214)
- **Derived IAST:** *Māheśvarī mahādevī mahālakṣmīr mṛḍapriyā*
- **Meaning:** *"The Great Goddess, the Great Devi, the Great Lakshmi, dear to Mrida (Shiva)."* Guru Karunamaya uses this line explicitly for job and business success *(arthika kashtalu)*.
- **Website audio:** `Maheshvari-mahadivi-mahalakshmi-rmrudapriya-2.mp3`, `job.English.m4a`
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/job-business](https://srimeru.org/job-business/) *(the index's `/jobbusiness/` link 404s; `/job-business/` resolves)*

### Remedy 14 — Back Pain
- **Stanza:** **106/1** — `Muladharanbujarudha panchavaktrasdhi sanpdhita`
- **Full stanza 106:** `Muladharanbujarudha panchavaktrasdhi sanpdhita / Ankushadi praharana varadadi nishevita – 106`
- **PDF:** p. 11, [Line 380](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md#L380)
- **Derived IAST:** *Mūlādhārāmbujārūḍhā pañcavaktrāsthi-saṃsthitā*
- **Meaning:** *"Seated on the Mūlādhāra lotus, established in the five-faced (pañcavaktra) bone structure."* The `asthi` (bone) and Mūlādhāra/base-of-spine imagery make this the back/spinal selection.
- **Website audio:** `mula-dharambujaruḍha-panchavaktra_sthisaṃsthita.mp3`, `back-pain.unknown.mp4` (site reads "sthisaṃsthita"; source reads "sdi sanpdhita")
- **Page:** [srimeru.org/back-pain](https://srimeru.org/back-pain/)

## 4. Guru Karunamaya Physical-Healing Companions (from [05](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md))

Condition-specific physical methods documented from Guru Karunamaya's Telugu notice [`6xrK8ddgeHk`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xrK8ddgeHk), used **while listening** to the Samputikarana recitation:

| Condition | Physical Method |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Knee pain** | Crisscross both hands over the knees while listening/chanting. |
| **Skin diseases** | Sandalwood oil + turmeric + tulsi-leaf juice + honey; apply gently **downward** only (no back-and-forth rubbing). |
| **Paralysis** | Same paste **without honey**; apply gently downward. |
| **Depression, chronic illness, nerve weakness** | Copper glass of water + pinch of **vibhuti**, poured back and forth with a **copper uddharini (spoon)** while listening; the affected person drinks the water afterward. |

Additional Samputikarana inputs (kumkuma in *mriga mudra*, Day-1 sankalpa, 90 days, ~90 min/day, prasada on forehead) are catalogued in [05 Part 1](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md).
