# Stanzas Prescribed by Nanaji Patnaik (Verified Against Our PDF Edition)

This reference provides a dedicated, comprehensive catalog of the specific stanzas and names from **Shri Lalitha Sahasranamam** prescribed by astrologer and Lalitha *upasaka* **Sri Nanaji Patnaik**, verified against this repository's authoritative PDF edition ([01 — Transcription](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md) / [02 — PDF](02-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.pdf)).

For his general methodology, 45–48 minute chanting rules, and a detailed comparison with Sri Guru Karunamaya's 90-day *Samputikarana* method, see [06 — Nanaji Patnaik 45-minute stanza method & comparison](06-Nanaji_Patnaik_45min_Stanzas_and_Comparison.md).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Source edition fidelity:** Verbatim lines are quoted in the source edition's nonstandard Latin romanization (`docs/01-...` / `docs/02-...`). Derived IAST spellings, word separations, and astrological house correspondences are derived interpretations, not part of the source transcription.

> Spiritual, astrological, and health-related claims are attributed to Sri Nanaji Patnaik's public discourses, not medically or scientifically verified. These practices are spiritual sadhana and not a substitute for professional medical, psychological, financial, or legal advice.

## 1. Practice Formula and Core Rules

Nanaji Patnaik's method uses a direct **Eka-Nama / Stanza Japa** approach rather than encasing the entire 1,000 names:

1. **The Mantra Formula (`Om <Line/Name> Namaha`):**
   - Take the designated line or divine name from the hymn.
   - Prefix sacred syllable **`Om`**.
   - Convert the final name/line into the dative case ending with **`-ye Namaha`** or **`-yai Namaha`** (e.g., `Indragopa...` $\rightarrow$ `Om Indragopa Parikshipta Smaratunabha Janghikaye Namaha`).
2. **Chanting Duration:**
   - Chant the selected single formula continuously for **45 minutes** ([`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms)), **48 minutes** ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s)), or **1 hour** ([`WXM9dnBGHpA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXM9dnBGHpA)) each day.
   - A traditional cycle of **48 consecutive days** (*mandala*) is referenced in [`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) (`07:14–07:26`).
3. **Timing & Upadesha:**
   - No formal Guru initiation (*upadesha*) is required to recite Lalitha Sahasranamam ([`oALY0nEDtoU`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oALY0nEDtoU) `02:25`), though clear, accurate pronunciation is emphasized.
   - Best chanted in the **early morning (Brahma Muhurta)** or **evening / night** (Devi/Shakti deities are traditionally praised as especially potent at night).
4. **The "1 Hour vs. 23 Hours" Discipline:**
   - In [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`06:22–07:24`), Nanaji warns against chanting for 45 minutes and then spending the remaining 23 hours harboring worry, cynicism, or doubt (*"will this work?"*). Positive mental orientation and faith (*bhavana*) must be maintained throughout the day.
5. **Devotion and Visualization (*Bhavana*):**
   - Chant with affectionate devotion toward the Divine Mother ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) `05:55–06:56`), visualizing the specific divine form, limb, or quality described in the verse ([`WXM9dnBGHpA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXM9dnBGHpA) `08:15–09:04`).

## 2. Prescribed Stanzas by Life Area

### 1. Severe Debt Removal (*Rina Vimochana*)

*For heavy, crippling debts (`appulu ekkuvaga unnai`) that cause continuous anxiety.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Aparnaye Namaha}$$
- **Name Source:** Extracted from the sacred name **`Aparna`**.
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 15, stanza 145, line 2** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L506)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 145 Text:**
  ```text
  Maheshvari mahakali mahagrasa mahashana
  Aparna chandika chandamundashura nishudini – 145
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Aparṇā caṇḍikā caṇḍamuṇḍāsura-niṣūdinī*
- **Literal Meaning:** *Aparna* refers to Parvati performing severe tapas without even consuming a single falling leaf (*parna*), symbolizing total ascetic mastery and dissolving accumulated karmic debts.
- **Source Video & Discourse:** [`WjNtbkViSRE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WjNtbkViSRE) (`09:22–09:26`): *"For heavy debts (`appulu ekkuvaga unnai`), `Om Aparnaye Namaha` works wonderfully."*

### 2. Financial Constraints & Blocked Cashflow (*Dhana Labha*)

*For stalled receivables, blocked business payments, and afflictions to the astrological 2nd house of wealth.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Sampatkari Samarudha Sindura Vraja Sevitaye Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 5, stanza 25, line 1** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L125)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 25 Text:**
  ```text
  Sampat-kari samaruda sindhura-vraja sevita
  Asvarudadhishtitasva koti bhiravruta – 25
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Sampatkarī-samārūḍha-sindhura-vraja-sevitā*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"Served by an army of divine elephants led by Sampatkari Devi (the deity commanding all wealth and resources)."*
- **Astrological Correspondence:** The 2nd house (*dhana sthana*).
- **Source Videos & Discourses:**
  - [`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) (`01:44–02:51`): For unlocking stalled cashflow and reviving financial stability.
  - [`WjNtbkViSRE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WjNtbkViSRE) (`08:37`): Chanting 45–48 minutes daily to stimulate financial flow.

### 3. Wealth Multiplier & Sustainable Abundance (*Dhana Dhanya Vivardhini*)

*For expanding ethical earnings, business prosperity, and multiplying family resources on a righteous foundation.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Dharmadhara Dhanadhyaksha Dhanadhanya Vivardhinaye Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 16, stanza 165, line 1** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L567)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 165 Text:**
  ```text
  Dharmadhara dhanadhyaksha dhanadhanya vivardhini
  Viprapriya viprarupa vishvabhramana karini – 165
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Dharmādhārā dhanādhyakṣā dhanadhānya-vivardhinī*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"She who is the foundation of dharma, overseer/custodian of all wealth, and the multiplier of diverse wealth and food grains."*
- **Practical Note:** Nanaji stresses that *Dharma* comes first; chanting this line ensures wealth increases through righteous, peaceful avenues.
- **Source Video & Discourse:** [`WjNtbkViSRE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WjNtbkViSRE) (`08:41–09:00`).

### 4. Health, Healing & Relief from Chronic Disease (*Sarva Vyadhi Prashamani*)

*For general ailments, chronic conditions, and afflictions to the astrological 4th (comfort/chest) and 6th (disease/debts) houses.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Sarvavyadhi Prashamani Sarvamrityu Nivarini Agraganya Achintyarupa Kalikalmashanashine Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 12, stanza 112 line 2 – stanza 113 line 1** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L401-L403)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 112–113 Text:**
  ```text
  ...
  Sarvavyadhi prashamani sarvamrutyu nivarini – 112

  Agraganya chintyarupa kalikalmasha nashini
  ... – 113
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Sarvavyādhi-praśamanī sarvamṛtyu-nivāriṇī / Agragaṇyācintyarūpā kalikalmaṣanāśinī*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"Pacifier of all illnesses, averter of untimely death, foremost among all, of inconceivable form, destroyer of the impurities of Kali Yuga."*
- **Practical Guidance:**
  - Chant 45–48 minutes daily.
  - In [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`03:02–03:07`), he also suggests chanting the short protective mantra `Kreem Achyuta Ananta Govinda` three times before drinking water or eating if the Sanskrit line feels too long.
- **Source Videos & Discourses:**
  - [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`03:02–03:07`)
  - [`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) (`01:07–01:25`)

### 5. Job Success & Career Advancement (*Karma Sthana / 10th House*)

*For securing employment, promotion, professional recognition, and overcoming stagnation in career.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Manikya Makutakara Janudvaya Virajitaye Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 4, stanza 17, line 2** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L100)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 17 Text:**
  ```text
  Kameshagyata saobhagya mardavoru dvayanvita
  Manikya makuta kara janudvaya virajita – 17
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Māṇikya-makuṭākāra-jānudvaya-virājitā*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"She whose twin knees shine like crowns fashioned from radiant rubies."*
- **Astrological Body-Correspondence:** In classical Vedic astrology (*Kalapurusha Tatva*), the **knees (*janu*)** correspond directly to the **10th house (*Karma Sthana*)**, which governs career, profession, authority, and public status. By praising Devi's crowned knees, the 10th house is activated and purified.
- **Source Videos & Discourses:**
  - [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`02:32–02:41`): Specifically prescribes **45 minutes daily** of this mantra for job seekers.
  - [`WjNtbkViSRE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WjNtbkViSRE) (`04:31–05:29`): Explains the detailed astrological mechanics connecting knees to profession.

### 6. Business Recovery & Inner Peace Despite Wealth (*Indragopa*)

*For reviving a struggling business, or for individuals who possess material affluence but suffer from chronic lack of peace and happiness.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Indragopa Parikshipta Smaratunabha Janghikaye Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 4, stanza 18, line 1** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L102)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 18 Text:**
  ```text
  Endra-gopa parikshipta smaratunabha janghika
  Guda-gulpha kurma-prushta jayishnu prapadanvita – 18
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Indragopa-parikṣipta-smaratūṇābha-jaṅghikā*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"Whose shanks resemble the quivers of Kamadeva, adorned like scarlet indragopa beetles."*
- **Astrological Correspondence:** The shanks correspond to the **11th house** (*labha sthana* — profits, cash inflow, gains) and the **9th/5th house** connection to joy.
- **Remedy Pairing:** In [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`04:22–04:35`), he recommends pairing this japa with feeding *gongura* (sorrel leaves) or green fodder to a cow to accelerate business recovery.
- **Source Videos & Discourses:**
  - [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`04:22–04:35`)
  - [`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) (`02:52–04:52`)

### 7. Overcoming Fear, Timidity & Indecision (*Dhriti & Sahasa*)

*For dispelling hesitation, fear of failure, and weakness in the astrological 3rd house (courage, initiative, arms).*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Kanakangada Keyura Kamaniya Bhujanvitaye Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 4, stanza 13, line 1** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L87)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 13 Text:**
  ```text
  Kanakangada keyura kamaniya bhujanvita
  Ratnagre-veya chintaka lola mukta phalanvita – 13
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Kanakāṅgada-keyūra-kamanīya-bhujānvitā*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"Endowed with lovely arms adorned with golden bracelets and armlets."*
- **Astrological Body-Correspondence:** The arms (*bhuja*) correspond to the **3rd house (*Bhratru & Dhairya Sthana*)**, conferring courage, valor, and strength to initiate action without fear.
- **Source Video & Discourse:** [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`03:11–03:27`).

### 8. Mental Clarity & Inner Stability (*Manovikara Nivarana*)

*For controlling erratic thoughts, anxiety, depression, and cultivating an unshakeable, peaceful intellect.*

- **Mantra Formula:**
  $$\text{Om Manasvinyai Namaha}$$
- **Name Source:** Extracted from the name **`Manasvini`**.
- **Source PDF Location:** **p. 17, stanza 172, line 2** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L591)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 172 Text:**
  ```text
  Matruka varna rupini vidyadhatri shuvadaprada
  Manasvini manavati maheshi mangalakrutihi – 172
  ```
- **Derived IAST:** *Manasvinī mānavatī māheśī maṅgalākṛtiḥ*
- **Literal Meaning:** *Manasvini* means *"She who is supreme ruler of the mind, endowed with great intellect and self-control."*
- **Discourse Note:** Nanaji advises regarding one's own mind as a consecrated altar of the Mother, repeating this single name to banish erratic mental disturbances.
- **Source Video & Discourse:** [`WjNtbkViSRE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WjNtbkViSRE) (`02:43–03:11`).

### 9. Marriage & Marital Harmony (*Vivaha & Dampatya Sukha*)

*For resolving delays in marriage, removing marital discord, and fostering deep, affectionate companionship.*

- **Mantra Formulas:**
  1. For early marriage / auspicious alliance:
     $$\text{Om Kameshabaddha Mangalya Sutra Shobhita Kandharayai Namaha}$$
  2. For deep harmony, devotion, and understanding between spouses:
     $$\text{Om Shivakameshwaran-kastha Shiva Swadhina Vallabhayai Namaha}$$
- **Source PDF Locations:**
  - Formula 1: **p. 4, stanza 12, line 2** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L85)).
  - Formula 2: **p. 4, stanza 21, line 2** ([01](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md?plain=1#L112)).
- **Verbatim Stanza 12 & 21 Text:**
  ```text
  ...
  Kamesha bada mangalya sutra-shobhita kandhara – 12

  ...
  Shivakameshvarankasdha shiva svadhinavallabha – 21
  ```
- **Derived IAST:**
  - *Kāmeśabaddha-māṅgalya-sūtra-śobhita-kandharā* (*"Whose neck is adorned with the sacred mangalsutra tied by Kameshwara"*)
  - *Śivakāmeśvarāṅkasthā śiva-svādhīna-vallabhā* (*"Seated on the lap of Shiva-Kameshwara, who holds Her Lord completely enchanted by Her love"*)
- **Chanting Procedure:**
  - Chant for **48 minutes daily** ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) `05:38–05:55`) or **1 hour daily** ([`WXM9dnBGHpA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXM9dnBGHpA) `07:38–09:04`).
  - Visualize the divine union of Shiva and Shakti while chanting.

*For clearing chronic roadblocks and obtaining auspicious desires through the complete grace of Lalitha Devi.*

- **Verbatim Stanza 23 Text:**
  ```text
  Mahapadmatavi sansdha kadanba vanavasinee
  Sudhasagara madhyasdha kamakshi kamadaeini – 23
  ```

- **Derived IAST:** *Mahāpadmāṭavī-saṁsthā kadamba-vana-vāsinī / Sudhāsāgara-madhyasthā kāmākṣī kāmadāyinī*
- **Literal Meaning:** *"Dwelling in the great lotus forest, residing in the Kadamba grove, situated in the midst of the ocean of nectar, with loving eyes (*Kamakshi*), fulfiller of all desires (*Kamadayini*)."*
