## 2. Nanaji Patnaik's Process & Rules

1. **Formula (`Om <Line> Namaha`):** Take the specific *nama*/line from Lalitha Sahasranamam corresponding to the issue, prefix `Om`, and add the dative suffix `-ye/-yai Namaha` (e.g., `Om Manikya Makutakara Janudvaya Virajitaye Namaha`).
2. **Duration:** Chant that single mantra continuously for **45 minutes** ([`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms)), **48 minutes** ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s)), or **1 hour** ([`WXM9dnBGHpA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXM9dnBGHpA)) daily without abandoning it after a couple of days (`48 days` cited as a reference cycle in [`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) `07:14–07:26`).
3. **Timing & Eligibility:** No formal Guru *upadesha* is required for Lalitha Sahasranamam ([`oALY0nEDtoU`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oALY0nEDtoU) `02:25`), though correct pronunciation matters (`02:32–03:39`). Chant in the **early morning** or **evening/night** (Shakti deities are noted as especially responsive at night). Not a rigid *Kalasha* *deeksha*; if non-vegetarian food is eaten later, simply bathe again before chanting ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) `07:07–07:38`).
4. **Mental Discipline ("1 Hour vs. 23 Hours" Rule):** In [`lovnA629Bms`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lovnA629Bms) (`06:22–07:24`), Nanaji Patnaik warns that chanting for 45–60 minutes and then spending the other **23 hours** repeating negative doubts (*"will this work or not?"*) outweighs the mantra and blocks the *shatchakras* (especially the *anahata chakra*).
5. **Devotion & Visualization (*Bhavana*):** Chant with warmth and affection ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s) `05:55–06:56`), and visualize the scene/meaning described by the verse ([`WXM9dnBGHpA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXM9dnBGHpA) `08:15–09:04`).
6. **Astrology + Vastu + Charity (Optional Layer):** He frequently pairs the mantra with checking the afflicted astrological house/lord (e.g., 2nd house for wealth, 6th/4th for health, 10th for job, 11th/5th for happiness), planetary donations, Vastu checks, or selfless service.

## 4. Comparison: Guru Karunamaya vs. Nanaji Patnaik

| Dimension | Sri Guru Karunamaya (*Samputikarana*) | Sri Nanaji Patnaik (*Eka-Nama / Stanza Japa*) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Recitation Structure** | **Encased full text:** Chosen line recited **before and after every one of the 365 lines** of Lalitha Sahasranamam. | **Direct repetition:** Chosen line/name framed as `Om ... Namaha` and chanted repeatedly on its own. |
| **Daily Time Commitment** | **~90 minutes/day** (1h25m–1h38m at `1.0x`; shorter at `1.25x`–`1.40x`). | **45–48 minutes/day** (up to 1 hour). |
| **Cycle Length** | **90 consecutive days** (same place, same time; women may pause 3–5 days). | **48 days** referenced ([`VK3W8aFam0s`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VK3W8aFam0s)), or ongoing daily practice until resolved. |
| **Ritual Medium** | **Kumkuma puja** in *mriga mudra* on Yantra/photo/idol, applied as *prasada* afterward (plus special water/paste methods in [`6xrK8ddgeHk`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xrK8ddgeHk)), or passive audio listening. | Lamp + image/printout (or simple mental focus), devotional *bhavana*/visualization, plus optional astrological donations & Vastu. |
| **How Stanzas Are Mapped** | Primarily **literal/symbolic correspondence** (e.g., `17/2` knees -> knee joints; `18/1` shanks -> varicose veins) + Sri Vidya tradition (`53/2`, `33/2`, `112/2`). | Combines **literal meaning** (`145/2` *Aparna*, `165/1` *Dhanadhyaksha*, `112/2` *Sarvavyadhi*) with **astrological house–body mapping** (e.g., `17/2` knees -> 10th house job/profession; `18/1` -> business/11th-house joy). |
| **Mindset Rule** | State desire on **Day 1 only**; Day 2+ drop worry and practice **gratitude**. | **1 hour mantra vs. 23 hours doubt** rule: keep faith and positive mental focus throughout the day. |

### How They Complement Each Other Across Your 8 Issues
- **Overlapping selections:** Both are associated here with **`p. 12, 112/2`** (`Sarvavyadhi prashamani sarvamrutyu nivarini`) for **Health**, **`p. 4, 12/2`** for **Marriage**, and **`p. 4, 21/2`** for **Marital Harmony**. The `165/1` recommendation is sourced here to Nanaji; the Guru Karunamaya guide lists it as a separate thematic selection, not a verified channel recommendation.
- **Practical combination:** If you have **90 minutes**, run **Guru Karunamaya's Samputikarana** on your top priority issue (e.g., `53/2` or `33/2` or `112/2`); if you have **45 minutes** or want to address a second issue (like `145/2` *Om Aparnaye Namaha* for debt, `25/1` *Om Sampatkari...* for blocked cashflow, or `17/2` for career/10th house), **Nanaji Patnaik's 45-minute *Om ... Namaha* japa** provides a lighter, focused alternative.
