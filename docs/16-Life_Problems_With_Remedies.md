# Life Problems With Remedies — Master Directory

Single directory mapping **life problems → prescribed remedies** across this repository's two teaching sources:

- **Sri Guru Karunamaya / srimeru.org** — condition-indexed remedies (full catalog: [15](15-Srimeru_Guru_Karunamaya_Remedies_Catalog.md))
- **Sri Nanaji Patnaik** — 45–48 minute `Om ... Namaha` problem-specific japa (full catalog: [07](07-Stanzas_Prescribed_by_Nanaji_Patnaik.md), [14](14-Nanaji_Patnaik_Complete_Remedies_Reference.md))

Every Lalitha Sahasranamam stanza below is **cross-verified** against this edition ([01 — Transcription](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md) / [02 — PDF](02-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.pdf)); page and line references reflect the verified transcription. Remedies marked **non-LSN** use other scriptures, homas, or pujas and have no stanza entry.

> **Health/medical disclaimer:** These are spiritual practices attributed to their teachers. They are **not** medical advice and do not replace professional care — especially for cancer, paralysis, infertility, and neurological conditions.

## B. How to Choose a Method

| Situation | Recommended approach |
|-----------|---------------------|
| A specific body ailment named on srimeru.org | Use the **srimeru stanza** (short, one line) with its companion physical method; listen to the site audio if available. |
| Time-poor, need focused relief | **Nanaji Patnaik 45–48 min** single `Om ... Namaha` formula for 48 days. |
| Deep/complex karma, ample time | **Guru Karunamaya Samputikarana** (~90 min/day, 90 days) — see [04](04-Samputikarana_Process_and_Transcripts.md), [05](05-Eight_Life_Areas_Samputikarana_Guide.md). |
| Catastrophic emergency | **27× single-day parayanam** (Nanaji) and/or **Aghora Gauri / homa** rituals (srimeru). |
| Condition uses another scripture | Follow the **non-LSN** remedy (Sri Rudram, Durga Saptashati, Medha Suktham, Varahi, Gandharva Raja, homas). |

Universal rules shared by both sources: chant clearly with devotion (*bhavana*), keep the same time/place, pair with charity (*seva*), and avoid doubt during the day (Nanaji's "1 hour vs 23 hours" rule).
