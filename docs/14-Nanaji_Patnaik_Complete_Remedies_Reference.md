## Core Practice Methodology

### The Universal Mantra Formula
```
Om + [Stanza/Name in Dative Case] + Namaha
```
**Example:** `Indragopa parikshipta smaratunabha janghika` → `Om Indragopa Parikshipta Smaratunabha Janghikaye Namaha`

|----------------|----------|--------|
| Standard Daily Japa | **45 minutes** | `lovnA629Bms` |

| Deep Karmic Affliction | **1 hour** continuous + visualization | `WXM9dnBGHpA` |

### Eligibility & Timing
- **No Guru Upadesha Required** — Lalitha Sahasranamam is public and universal, composed by the Vagdevatas (`oALY0nEDtoU` 02:25)
- **Dietary Non-Dogmatism** — If non-vegetarian food consumed, bathe, change clothes, chant in evening (`VK3W8aFam0s` 07:07–07:38)
- **Optimal Times** — Early morning (Brahma Muhurta) or evening/night (Shakti heightened at night, `oALY0nEDtoU`)
- **Pronunciation** — Clear, deliberate, acoustically accurate Sanskrit phonetics stimulate nadis/chakras (`oALY0nEDtoU` 02:32–03:39)

### Psychological Discipline
1. **1 Hour vs 23 Hours Rule** (`lovnA629Bms` 06:22–07:24) — Do not nullify 45–60 min japa by spending 23 hours in doubt/worry; maintain faith (bhavana) all day
2. **Active Visualization (Rupadhyana)** (`WXM9dnBGHpA` 08:15–09:04) — Hold visual image of Mother's described form (crowned knees, adorned neck, radiant smile) while chanting
3. **Premapurvaka Bhavana** (`VK3W8aFam0s` 05:55–06:56) — Chant with warmth/affection of a child calling Mother, not mechanical duty
4. **Charitable Counterweight (Seva)** (`WXM9dnBGHpA`) — Pair japa with feeding cows (gongura/green fodder), feeding hungry, supporting marriages/education of needy

#### Suggested Practice for Migraine
- **Primary Formula:** Choose **one** based on symptom profile: 10A for chronic/neurological, 10B for vascular/throbbing, 10C for sinus/facial/ocular.
- **Duration:** 45–48 min daily (standard), 1 hr for severe chronic cases.
- **Timing:** **Night/evening preferred** (Shakti heightened at night, `oALY0nEDtoU`); moon-energy (*Chandranibha*) naturally cooling.
- **Visualization (Rupadhyana):** Cool white moonlight permeating the specific region (forehead / crown / face).
- **Seva Pairing:** Feeding cooling foods (buttermilk, cucumber, coconut water) to cows/poor; donating white clothes/rice on Mondays (Moon day).
- **Medical Disclaimer:** Spiritual sadhana **complements**, does not replace, medical treatment for migraine/neurological conditions. Consult a neurologist.
