## 2. Documented Process of Samputikarana

### A. What "Samputikarana" Means

1. **Core definition (encasing / sandwiching):**
   - In [`WrmBAEpK9DA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrmBAEpK9DA), Guru Karunamaya defines *Samputikarana* as enclosing a central mantra or divine name on both sides with another mantra or seed syllables (*bijas*), optionally in **anuloma–viloma** (ascending then descending/reverse order, e.g., `Aim Kleem Souh ... Souh Kleem Aim` in Bala mantra, or `Eim Hreem Shreem Prakruthyey Namaha Shreem Hreem Eim` for Lakshmi Ashtottaram). Placing the framing element before and after multiplies the potency of the middle mantra.
   - In the English Playlist descriptions and [`uXJV5tK4vBw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXJV5tK4vBw) / [`CE0lqh7sJUE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CE0lqh7sJUE), he applies this to **Lalitha Sahasranama**: a single problem-specific line from the hymn is placed **before and after every one of the 365 lines**, so the entire hymn is recited/heard with that line encasing each line.
2. **Safety & Guru guidance:**
   - In [`WrmBAEpK9DA`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WrmBAEpK9DA) (`01:24–01:41`), he compares combining mantras/bijas to mixing chemicals in a chemistry lab: arbitrary combinations without a Guru's guidance can backfire ("you may get into [a] blast"). For the Lalitha Sahasranama Samputikarana series, the 365 pre-recorded audios are prepared by the center so practitioners can safely follow along without assembling arbitrary bija combinations themselves.

### B. How the Chanting Is Structured (Verified Against Playlist Audio & Repository Text)

- **Chanting pattern:** Verified from the chanting transcripts of [`LpibASlwSz0`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LpibASlwSz0) (`01:54–03:56`) and [`c53p1rGfirM`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c53p1rGfirM) (`00:18–02:56`):
  ```text
  [Target Line]  ->  [Line 1 of LSN]  ->  [Target Line]
  [Target Line]  ->  [Line 2 of LSN]  ->  [Target Line]
  [Target Line]  ->  [Line 3 of LSN]  ->  [Target Line]
  ... (repeated across all 365 lines)
  ```
  When the recitation reaches the target line itself (verified in `LpibASlwSz0` at `02:20–02:36`), the line is chanted three times in a row (before-frame, line itself, after-frame).
- **Duration per session:** ~90 minutes (1 hour 25 minutes to 1 hour 38 minutes across the English Playlist videos; stated as "one and a half hours" in `uXJV5tK4vBw` and `CE0lqh7sJUE`).
- **Line-count note vs. this repository's edition:** Guru Karunamaya refers to **365 lines** of Lalitha Sahasranama. In this repository's [01 — Transcription](01-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.md) and [02 — PDF](02-Lalitha_Sahasra_Namamulu_English.pdf), there are 183 two-line stanzas (**366 lines total**), where lines 1–365 contain the names (stanza 1 line 1 through stanza 183 line 1) and the 366th line (`stanza 183, line 2`: `Yvam shri lalita devya namnam sahasrakam jaguh`, p. 18) is the concluding statement.
- **Line lookup tool:** The playlist descriptions link to [Samputeekarana PDF Generator](https://lsn-generator.vercel.app/), which indexes lines `1-1` through `183-2` by stanza/line number.

### C. Step-by-Step Daily Practice Process (English Playlist + Podcast Guidance)

1. **Select the target line / audio:** Choose the video/audio corresponding to the specific life issue or inner quality (for example, [`uXJV5tK4vBw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXJV5tK4vBw) for marital bond `[Sloka 14-1]`, [`c53p1rGfirM`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=c53p1rGfirM) for prosperity `[Sloka 33-2]`, etc.).
2. **Sankalpa (Day 1 only, then surrender):**
   - From [`CE0lqh7sJUE`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CE0lqh7sJUE) (`44:44–45:26`, `46:48–46:59`) and [`HXlWElCdSkw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HXlWElCdSkw) (`00:22–01:31`, `02:47–02:55`): Hold your specific desire/prayer only on **Day 1** ("filing the application" before the Divine Mother).
   - From **Day 2 onward**, stop dwelling on the problem/desire—trust that Mother has received it, and chant with **gratitude** ("Thank you, Amma, for giving me this problem as an opportunity to recite Your names for 90 days").
3. **Altar setup & Kumkuma Puja (from [`uXJV5tK4vBw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXJV5tK4vBw) `00:57–01:36` and [`LpibASlwSz0`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LpibASlwSz0) `00:57–01:36`):**
   - Have an idol, photo of Devi, or Sri Chakra / Yantra in front of you.
   - Hold kumkuma between your fingers in *mriga mudra* (demonstrated on video; explicitly noted in Telugu companion [`6xrK8ddgeHk`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xrK8ddgeHk) `01:38`) and offer small pinches onto Mother's feet or the Yantra while listening to or chanting along with the audio.
   - **What NOT to do:** Do **not** wipe/clean your fingers together or push the kumkuma off with your fingernail—Guru Karunamaya states this is *"absolutely wrong"*.
4. **Playback speed & passive listening option (from English Playlist descriptions & `CE0lqh7sJUE`):**
   - **Learning:** Normal speed (`1.0x`).
   - **Meditation / daily chanting:** Earlier uploads recommend `1.25x`; later uploads (e.g., `c53p1rGfirM`, `iiJTRb6dbsY`, `HLcjj5eSzbI`) recommend `1.40x` for daily chanting.
   - **If unable to chant/do puja actively:** Keep the video/audio playing continuously to maintain spiritual vibrations, or listen attentively (for physical ailments such as knee pain in `CE0lqh7sJUE` `47:35–48:15`, he notes listening to the audio while crisscrossing one's hands over the knees).
5. **90-day discipline & menstrual exception:**
   - Perform the practice daily for **90 days without a break**, as far as possible at the **same place and same time** each day.
   - **Exception for women:** In [`uXJV5tK4vBw`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uXJV5tK4vBw) (`01:21–01:28`), he states: *"For female[s], you can stop for 3 to 5 days if you are not comfortable"* (noted as 3 to 4 days in `LpibASlwSz0`).
6. **Closing / Prasada:**
   - At the end of the daily puja, take the offered kumkuma and apply it to your forehead as **prasada**.

*(Note: The later Telugu overview [`6xrK8ddgeHk`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6xrK8ddgeHk) also describes two condition-specific procedures—a sandalwood oil/turmeric/tulsi juice/honey paste for skin conditions or paralysis without honey, and copper-glass vibhuti water poured back and forth while listening for depression/chronic illness/nerve weakness—which are not mentioned in the English Playlist intro.)*
